# GRC103 – Risk Monitoring and Management Reporting

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the International Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics Academy (ICDFA) GRC103 Practical Laboratory.

The exercise focused on monitoring cybersecurity risk indicators, analysing performance trends, developing a risk register and proposing treatment actions for the fictional organisation, Northbridge Services.

**Reporting Period:** January – March 2026

## Objectives
- Develop an Excel risk monitoring dashboard.
- Calculate and classify key risk indicators.
- Identify emerging risks through trend analysis.
- Develop a risk register and treatment action plan.
- Prepare a management report with recommendations.

## Key Findings
All five monitoring indicators were classified as Red in March.

| Indicator | March Result |
|---|---:|
| Monitoring Coverage | 90% |
| Timely Critical-Alert Review | 70% |
| Restore-Test Success | 80% |
| Overdue High-Risk Actions | 40% |
| Unauthorised File Changes | 5 |

Four priority risks were identified: monitoring blind spots, delayed alert review, failed restoration tests and overdue risk treatment actions.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel – Dashboard, calculations and charts.
- Microsoft Word – Management reporting.
- Risk Assessment – Risk scoring, classification and treatment planning.

## Deliverables
- Risk monitoring dashboard and trend charts.
- Risk register with scores and ratings.
- Risk treatment action plan.
- Management report with findings and recommendations.

## Key Learning Outcomes
- Cybersecurity risk monitoring and reporting.
- Risk indicator analysis and classification.
- Risk assessment and treatment planning.
- Communicating findings to management.
